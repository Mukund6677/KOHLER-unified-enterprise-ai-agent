KOHLER HR Policy

Employees are entitled to 24 paid leave days per year.

Employees must submit leave requests through the HR system.

Managers can view team-level employee information.

HR Admins can access employee records and modify HR records.

General Managers can view company-level HR information.

Employee personal information must not be disclosed to users without appropriate permissions.

Performance reviews are confidential and accessible only to authorized managers and HR personnel.
